06 / About

Twenty years of hard calls. Now written down.

Apto Strategic Consulting is Juan R. Cuellar. Army veteran. Immigrant. Educator. Writer. Two decades leading in places where a bad decision had real consequences, and the last several years building with AI, hands on the keyboard.

Apto no longer takes consulting clients. The work now is teaching and writing: courses, frameworks, free tools, a weekly newsletter, and a book on the way. Same material, in a format that reaches more people and does not depend on whether there is an opening on my calendar.

No products are resold here and no commissions are collected, so when AI is the wrong answer, you will hear that. Most of what organizations need is not exotic. It is the ordinary things done well, explained in language a normal person can follow.

Apto comes from the Latin aptus: fit, capable, suited for purpose. Today it also names the work: AI-Powered Transformation and Optimization.
