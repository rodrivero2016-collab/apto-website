02 / The Solution

Four ways to start.

No long reports. No new software to learn from scratch. We look at the work your team already does, find the parts a computer can handle, and set it up with you. Start small, add more later.

03 / The Steps

Get started with four steps.

Most projects run 30 to 90 days. You will know what is happening at every stage, and everything built belongs to you.

Assess

A conversation about how the work really gets done, where the time disappears, and what has already been tried.

Map

A short plan in plain English: what to fix first, what it costs, and what you get back. You decide what moves forward.

Build

It gets set up and connected. You watch it work on your real, everyday tasks before anyone calls it finished.

Hand Off

Training, simple written instructions, and support afterward. The goal is your team running it comfortably on their own.

08 / About

Twenty years of hard calls. Now pointed at AI.

Apto Strategic Consulting is led by Juan R. Cuellar. Army veteran. Immigrant. Educator. Two decades leading in places where a bad decision had real consequences, and the last several years building with AI, hands on the keyboard.
