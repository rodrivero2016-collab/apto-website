03 / How It Works

Four steps. No mystery.

Most engagements run 30 to 90 days. You know what's happening at every stage, and you own everything I build.

Assess

A working session on how your business actually operates. Where time leaks. What it's costing. What you've already tried.

Map

A short, plain-English roadmap: what to fix first, which tools, what it costs, what you get back. You decide what moves forward.

Build

I set it up and connect it. You watch it work on your real data, with your real workflow, before we call it done.

Hand Off

Training, documentation, and a support window. The goal is you running it without me, not a permanent dependency.
